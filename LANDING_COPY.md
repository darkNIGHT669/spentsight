## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no trial, no login required. You enter your tools and
get your results immediately. Email is optional and only asked after you've
seen your audit.

**Q: How do you know the pricing is accurate?**
A: Every number in the audit engine is pulled directly from official vendor
pricing pages — cursor.com/pricing, anthropic.com/pricing, github.com/features/copilot,
and so on. We note the verification date on each number. No AI is used in
the pricing math — only in the optional summary paragraph.

**Q: Will you use my data to sell me something?**
A: The audit is genuinely free and useful on its own. If your audit shows
significant savings (> $300/month), we'll mention Credex — a service that
sells discounted AI credits. You're never pressured to engage with it.

**Q: What's Credex?**
A: Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude,
ChatGPT Enterprise — from companies that overforecast their usage and want
to offload unused capacity. If your team spends a lot on AI tools, buying
through Credex can save an additional 20–40% on top of plan optimisations.

**Q: What if my stack is already optimised?**
A: We'll tell you honestly. "You're spending well" is a valid audit result.
You can still sign up to be notified when pricing changes make new savings
available for your specific stack.
